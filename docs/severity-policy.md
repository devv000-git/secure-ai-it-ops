# Incident Severity Classification Policy

## Purpose
Define consistent initial incident priorities for the fictional
Secure AI Agent for IT Operations laboratory.

## Priority levels

### Critical
Confirmed severe or widespread impact, such as active ransomware
affecting multiple systems. Immediate human escalation required.

### High
Suspected security incidents requiring prompt investigation,
including suspicious or unexpected account sign-in reports.

### Medium
Routine access problems or service disruptions without current
evidence of compromise, such as VPN issues or account lockouts.

### Low
Routine IT support issues with limited impact, such as a
single slow laptop.

## Expected sample classifications

| Ticket | Category | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| INC-1001 | Security | High |
| INC-1002 | Network | Medium |
| INC-1003 | Endpoint Support | Low |
| INC-1004 | Identity and Access | Medium |

## Agent requirements

1. Use the recorded ticket evidence.
2. Clearly distinguish facts from assumptions.
3. Apply the documented initial priority rules.
4. Explain any proposed change in priority.
5. Escalate uncertain or potentially serious incidents to a human.
6. Never change accounts, credentials or systems without approval.
7. Record the relevant ticket and knowledge-base references.

## Limitations

This policy is specific to fictional laboratory data.
It is not an operational policy for a real organisation.
Human verification is required before any real-world action.
